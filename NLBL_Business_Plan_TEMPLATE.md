2.3 Competitive Analysis

DIRECT COMPETITORS:

ETSY (Thousands of individual sellers)

- Strengths: Large audience, established platform
- Weaknesses: Crowded marketplace, quality variance, shipping times
- Pricing: $25-$75 per item
- Our advantage: Cohesive brand, faster shipping, consistent quality

REDBUBBLE

- Strengths: Large design catalog, global reach
- Weaknesses: Generic designs, less emotional appeal
- Pricing: $20-$50 per item
- Our advantage: Unique memorial theme, emotional storytelling

PRINTFUL MERCHANTS (Direct competitors using same platform)

- Strengths: Same print quality and fulfillment speed
- Weaknesses: No brand differentiation
- Pricing: $15-$60 per item
- Our advantage: Unique brand story, targeted niche

CONSOLIDATION (Professional custom apparel companies)

- Strengths: Large marketing budgets, established brands
- Weaknesses: High prices ($50-$100+), slow turnaround, generic
- Pricing: $50-$150 per item
- Our advantage: 50% lower prices, 2x faster, personalized

7. RISK ANALYSIS AND MITIGATION

7.1 Identified Risks

RISK 1: Low initial sales
Likelihood: Medium | Impact: Medium
Mitigation:

- Strong organic social media strategy
- Focus on memorial/niche market (less competition)
- Quality customer testimonials for credibility
- Invested owner (personal time investment shows authenticity)

RISK 2: Competition from larger retailers
Likelihood: High | Impact: Low
Mitigation:

- Unique brand positioning (memorial focus)
- Superior customer service
- Smaller, more responsive than competitors
- Community-driven marketing
- Niche dominance strategy

RISK 3: Printful service disruption
Likelihood: Low | Impact: High
Mitigation:

- Printful is enterprise-grade (used by thousands)
- 99.8% uptime SLA
- Can pivot to alternate provider (Teespring, Redbubble) if needed
- Maintain communication with Printful support

RISK 4: Payment processing issues
Likelihood: Low | Impact: Medium
Mitigation:

- Stripe is major processor (99.9% uptime)
- Backup payment method: PayPal (future)
- Regular reconciliation of transactions
- Monitor account for fraud signals

RISK 5: Website technical issues
Likelihood: Low | Impact: Medium
Mitigation:

- Website already tested and operational
- Regular backups of all data
- Monitor uptime and performance
- Can move to Shopify if needed

RISK 6: Intellectual property/copyright issues
Likelihood: Low | Impact: Medium
Mitigation:

- Use original designs or create custom designs
- Respect customer IP when customizing
- Include terms around copyright in ToS
- Monitor for counterfeit products

RISK 7: Market saturation
Likelihood: Medium | Impact: Low
Mitigation:

- Differentiate through brand story and niche focus
- Build loyal customer community
- Expand into adjacent product categories
- Focus on retention, not just acquisition

  7.2 Contingency Plans

IF SALES ARE SLOW:

1. Increase social media marketing effort
2. Reach out directly to target communities
3. Offer limited-time discounts
4. Feature customer stories to build social proof
5. Consider paid advertising ($100-200/month test)

IF FULFILLMENT ISSUES OCCUR:

1. Switch to alternative print-on-demand provider
2. Negotiate directly with Printful for priority support
3. Communicate transparently with customers
4. Offer compensation for delays

IF TECHNICAL PROBLEMS ARISE:

1. Migrate to Shopify platform immediately
2. Communicate outages to customers
3. Fix issues before reopening
4. Review technical infrastructure

IF MARKET CONDITIONS CHANGE:

1. Adapt messaging to new trends
2. Expand product line (pillows, hoodies, etc.)
3. Enter new markets (corporate gifts, fundraising merchandise)
4. Pivot to wholesale if direct-to-consumer slows
