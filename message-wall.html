Legacy Poem • R.J. & T-Mainney

Dog tags humming purple flames, the sky bends beneath your names. Under bleeding roses, forged in mist, we chase the thunder of your eternal ride.

R.J., your bleeding rose beat over a Moto imported from Italy — a heartbeat that still roars through the Utah winds you owned. T-Mainney, your tags clatter warnings and blessings; November 21, twenty-two years gone but forever close.

We paint the night with neon petals and cruise through every cosmic portal just to hear your laughter echo. The journey lives in us, and every soul that wears these threads carries your light.

“No Limits Boy's Crew” — always riding, always remembered.
