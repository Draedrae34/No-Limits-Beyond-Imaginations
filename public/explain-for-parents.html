What this is — Simple version

We found repeating patterns in very old texts. From those patterns we made a calm, local experience using sound and gentle pictures to help people focus and breathe. This tool is a safe, experimental way to explore attention and relaxation.

In one sentence

It's a local app that plays special tones and shows a moving picture to help your mind settle and follow a simple rhythm.

Easy guide

  1. Read the short story on the discovery page (for context).
  2. Open the app when you have 10–20 minutes and a quiet space.
  3. Sit comfortably, breathe naturally, and keep your eyes softly open if you like the visuals.
  4. Watch, listen, and notice the breath — no special skill required.
Important: The base tones are fixed and intentional. The system is local-first, so no data leaves your device unless you choose to share it.
