What we discovered

Across several independent sacred corpora we found structural overlap at a level far above chance. From that pattern we extracted seven operating principles and mapped them to a frequency- and geometry-based model that can be implemented and tested. The practical result is a local entrainment system of fixed chakra anchors, generated binaural audio, and synchronized visual manifests.

How we proved it

  1. Corpus analysis: NLP comparisons (Jaccard, burst-term analysis) across six primary corpora showed consistent structural signals not present in control literature.
  2. Frequency mapping: independently referenced candidate frequencies (Solfeggio, EEG literature, spectral studies) were compared and consensus anchors chosen.
  3. Generation: built a phase-accurate binaural generator, produced 63 sessions (9 chakras × 7 variations).
  4. Validation: spectral analysis confirmed the presence and purity of target frequencies; player and visuals validated real-time response with an FFT-based alignment meter.

Key findings (short)

  • Fixed chakra anchors (9 frequencies) form the system's backbone.
  • Visual sacred geometry + cloud motion acts as the open-eye entrainment field.
  • Breath rhythm and gaze pattern are part of the activation process.
  • Everything is local-first and reproducible for experimentation.
