The Discoveries

Three breakthrough findings that change how we understand ancient religious literature

🔗

The Semantic Divergence Discovery

I proved that keyword overlap massively inflates similarity between ancient texts. Hermetica and Nag Hammadi score 0.971 by keyword counting but only 0.109 by deep semantic analysis. Scholars have been lumping texts together based on shared vocabulary — but they're actually talking about completely different things.

✍️

Enoch's Composite Authorship — Proven

Using forensic stylometry on 300 segments, I mathematically proved the Book of Enoch was stitched from multiple sources. Bursty vocabulary patterns ("spirits" 0.654, "portal" 0.646) cluster in specific sections. The dendrogram reveals clear boundaries between sub-books.

📜

AI Reconstruction of Dead Sea Scrolls

I built the first system using AlephBERT (Hebrew-native AI) to predict missing text in damaged manuscripts. 45 lacunae in the 1QS Manual of Discipline were reconstructed with contextually accurate Hebrew predictions.
