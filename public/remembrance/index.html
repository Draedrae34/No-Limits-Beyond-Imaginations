R.J. N T-Mainney — I Did This For You

I still look for you in the quiet, in the static between heartbeats, in the places where the world goes soft and the sky feels thin. Some nights I swear I hear your laughter riding the thunder, see your shadows dancing in the neon rain. I built this wall because I couldn't hold you here— so I made a place where the world could. If love could bend time, I'd trade every tomorrow for one more yesterday. But you taught me: grief is just love with nowhere to go, and memory is the only way to keep you close. So I write your names in starlight, I speak to you in the dark, I promise— as long as I breathe, you will not be lost. This is my forever. This is my surrender. This is my way of saying I'll see you again— but until then, I'll keep building you a home in the stars.
