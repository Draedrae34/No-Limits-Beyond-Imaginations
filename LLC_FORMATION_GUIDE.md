## Step-by-Step Filing Process (When You Have $100)

### PHASE 1: Register Business Name (When You Have $50-100)

**What to do:**

1. Visit your state's Secretary of State website
2. Fill out "Articles of Organization" form
3. Pay filing fee ($50-100, varies by state)
4. Get Certificate of Formation (they email it)
5. Takes 1-5 business days

**All state websites here:**
https://www.sba.gov/business-guide/launch/register-your-business

### PHASE 2: Get EIN from IRS (FREE - Do This Now)

1. Go to: https://onlineservices.irs.gov
2. Fill out SS-4 form (15 minutes)
3. Get EIN immediately
4. Print the confirmation letter
5. Cost: $0

### PHASE 3: Open Business Bank Account (Free or $0 minimum)

_Do this AFTER you get your EIN_

1. Go to your bank (or online bank like Stripe, Square)
2. Bring:
   - Certificate of Formation
   - EIN confirmation letter
   - ID
   - Operating Agreement (yours works great)
3. Open business checking account
4. Cost: Usually free or $0 minimum balance

## Tax Considerations (Before You Launch)

### Federal Taxes (FREE to set up):

1. Get EIN (FREE at irs.gov) ✅
2. Choose tax classification:
   - **Single-Member LLC**: Taxed as sole proprietor (simplest)
   - **Multi-Member LLC**: Taxed as partnership
   - **Elect S-Corp**: Later, when you have significant income ($60k+)
3. File Schedule C (Solo 1040) annually (FREE form)

### State Taxes:

- **Sales Tax**: Collect if shipping to your state
- **Business Income Tax**: Varies by state
- **Sales Tax Nexus**: Since Printful ships from multiple locations, you may owe this
  - **Action**: Check your state's nexus requirements
  - **Cost to research**: FREE

### Quarterly Estimated Taxes (When Revenue Starts):

- Pay quarterly taxes on profits
- Form: 1040-ES (FREE)
- Due: Apr 15, Jun 15, Sep 15, Jan 15
- Amount: Estimate your annual profit ÷ 4

## What NOT to Do (Avoid These Costs)

❌ Don't use expensive LLC formation services ($200-500):

- LegalZoom, Rocket Lawyer, etc.
- They charge premium fees
- You can do it for 1/5 the cost yourself

❌ Don't form in Delaware or Nevada if you're a solo business:

- Higher annual fees
- No real benefit without multiple owners
- Wait until you scale

❌ Don't over-engineer it before launch:

- Complex corporate structure - not needed yet
- Trademark your brand name now (FREE search at USPTO.gov)
- File actual trademark later ($300-500) when you have cash

❌ Don't hire expensive lawyers right now:

- You don't need them for basic LLC
- Use free templates
- Get lawyer only if issues arise (contracts, disputes)
