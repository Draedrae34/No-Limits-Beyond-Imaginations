## Quick steps for Facebook packaging
1. Zip `remembrance/photos/` & `Logo/logo.png` as `legacy-launch-media.zip`.
2. Save in `All No Limits Secret Keys/` folder or `uploads/` for easy access from your phone when posting.
3. In Facebook Creator Studio / mobile app, attach the zip/selected images and overlay text using the copy from the launch kit.
4. After publishing, pin the post to the top of your page.
